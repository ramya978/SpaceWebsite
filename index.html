Welcome to SPACE 1

An emerging initiative dedicated to space-science education, cosmic discovery and future-focused innovation.

  • Learn. Accessible space-science education for everyone.
  • Engage. Scientific engagement grounded in evidence.
  • Experience. Immersive ways to feel the wonder of the Universe.
  • Inspire. A new generation of learners, researchers and innovators.

Our Vision and Mission

Vision
  • Our vision is to develop SPACE 1 into a globally connected centre for space-science education, cosmic experiences and innovation.
Mission
  • Our mission is to encourage scientific curiosity, make space-related knowledge more accessible, support collaborative learning and explore emerging technologies that may shape the future of space exploration.

Explore the Universe

From the formation of stars and galaxies to the search for distant planets and the possibility of future human settlements beyond Earth, the Universe presents extraordinary opportunities for discovery. SPACE 1 aims to make these subjects engaging through educational content, interactive experiences and learning programmes grounded in scientific evidence.
