About SPACE 1

SPACE 1, the International Centre for Advanced Space Science and Cosmic Research, links education, public engagement and innovation. Our long-term vision includes:

  • Cosmic Experience Centre. An immersive space for the public.
  • Planetarium. The night sky and the Universe on a full dome.
  • Educational facilities. Places to teach and learn space science.
  • Research initiatives. Collaborative projects with partners.

“Building India's Future in Space Science, Research and Cosmic Exploration.”

I envision an India that stands shoulder to shoulder with the world's leading spacefaring nations, not merely as a participant in global space exploration, but as a pioneer in scientific discovery, technological innovation and the development of new frontiers of human knowledge.

SPACE 1 was born from this vision.

My dream is to create an institution that goes beyond conventional space education and becomes a centre for cosmic exploration, advanced learning, scientific research and international collaboration. I believe India's greatest strength lies in its intellectual capital. Our young generation possesses extraordinary potential. What they need are world-class facilities, access to advanced technologies, meaningful research opportunities and an ecosystem that allows them to compete and collaborate at the highest international levels.

Our long-term vision extends into some of the most transformative frontiers of science and technology, including:
  • Advanced Space Research
  • Space Medicine and Human Health
  • Interstellar Exploration: Asteroid and Extraterrestrial Resource Research
  • Planetary Science and Cosmic Discovery
  • Future Space Technologies

SPACE 1 will be developed through a structured, five-year, phase-wise implementation programme, commencing in January 2027 and progressing through December 2032, with clearly defined milestones across infrastructure, education, research and technological development.

The initiative will be governed, managed and executed by a highly experienced multidisciplinary team comprising distinguished space scientists, academicians, astronauts, professors and subject-matter experts, including professionals who have contributed to space missions, scientific programmes and aerospace initiatives in India and internationally.

Their collective expertise, institutional knowledge and global exposure will provide the scientific leadership, academic direction and strategic oversight required to establish SPACE 1 as a centre of excellence in cosmic education, advanced space research and future space technologies.

I see a future where Indian researchers contribute to international space missions, where our institutions collaborate with leading global universities and scientific organisations, where Indian innovations influence the emerging space economy, and where the next generation of space scientists, engineers, medical researchers and explorers emerge from our own soil.

With vision and purpose, Jayani Bennheim
Director & Chief visionary

Our Journey

SPACE 1 is being developed through a phased approach, beginning with educational planning, online learning initiatives and the establishment of academic and scientific relationships. Future phases may include interactive exhibitions, specialised laboratories, research programmes and a dedicated cosmic experience campus. Each stage will depend on appropriate expertise, resources, partnerships and approvals.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is SPACE 1?

An emerging space-science education, experience and innovation initiative.

Can students enrol in courses?

Yes, once programmes are formally launched and enrolment details are published.

Will SPACE 1 offer recognised certifications?

Certification arrangements will be stated for each programme. External accreditation or certification by another institution will be claimed only where formally authorised.

Will SPACE 1 establish a university?

A university is part of the longer-term vision, subject to feasibility, regulatory approvals and the requirements for higher-education institutions.
