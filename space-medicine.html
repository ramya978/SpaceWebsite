Space Medicine

Overview

A specialised field of medical science that studies how the space environment affects the human body and mind.

As missions reach the Moon, Mars and beyond, space medicine helps to:

  • Protect. Safeguard astronaut health.
  • Perform. Improve human performance.
  • Innovate. Develop new approaches to healthcare in extreme environments.
Our outlook At SPACE 1, we envision medical science and space exploration advancing together, so humanity can travel farther into the cosmos safely and sustainably.

Space Medicine

Our Research Focus

Seven areas that together cover the body, the mind and the technology that cares for both.

01

Human Physiology in Space

Effects of microgravity, for better astronaut health and mission readiness.

  • Muscles
  • Bones
  • Cardiovascular
  • Balance
02

Space Radiation and Human Health

Biological effects of cosmic radiation and solar particle events.

  • Radiation protection
  • Risk assessment
  • Long-mission safety
03

Aerospace and Astronaut Medicine

Medical care before, during and after human spaceflight.

  • Medical screening
  • Health monitoring
  • Preventive care
  • Flight protocols
04

Space Biology and Human Adaptation

How living organisms respond to the space environment.

  • Cellular function
  • Immunity
  • Metabolism
05

Mental Health and Human Performance

Well-being on long missions in confined, challenging environments.

  • Sleep
  • Stress
  • Cognition
  • Isolation
06

Medical Technologies for Space Exploration

Care systems for spacecraft and future planetary habitats.

  • Remote diagnostics
  • Compact devices
  • Telemedicine
  • Autonomous monitoring
  • Emergency systems
07

Moon and Mars Mission Health

The medical challenges of extended human missions.

  • Life support
  • Nutrition
  • Injury prevention
  • Emergency care
  • Long-term adaptation
